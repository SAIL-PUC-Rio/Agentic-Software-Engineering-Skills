# U9 — Definition

[Table 7, p. 8, row U9 — User education & Training](../../1-s2.0-S0164121224000980-main.pdf#page=8) introduces its definitions with “Addressing this concern involves specifying”:

> the need to provide user education and training on the limitations of the ML-enabled system and how to interpret its outputs

## When to analyze it

Analyze User education & Training when discussing [Ensure the credibility of predictions](../../perspectives/user-experience/README.md#ensure-the-credibility-of-predictions) in the [User Experience perspective](../../perspectives/user-experience/). In the PerSpecML workflow, inspect the concern and its available relationships, determine whether it applies to the system, and then specify it if applicable. The task assignment identifies where to have this specification discussion; it does not require waiting until implementation.

## Source differences

Table 7 uses “User education & Training”; Figure 2 uses “User education & training”. The table's capitalization is retained.
