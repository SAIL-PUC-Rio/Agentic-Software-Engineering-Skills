# M11 — Definition

[Table 9, p. 9, row M11 — Interpretability & Explainability](../../1-s2.0-S0164121224000980-main.pdf#page=9) introduces its definitions with “Addressing this concern involves specifying”:

> the need to understand reasons for the model inferences. The model might need to be able to summarize the reasons for its decisions. Other related concerns such as transparency, may apply

## When to analyze it

Analyze Interpretability & Explainability when discussing [Evaluate other quality characteristics](../../perspectives/model/README.md#evaluate-other-quality-characteristics) in the [Model perspective](../../perspectives/model/). In the PerSpecML workflow, inspect the concern and its available relationships, determine whether it applies to the system, and then specify it if applicable. The task assignment identifies where to have this specification discussion; it does not require waiting until implementation.

## Source differences

Table 9 uses “Interpretability & Explainability”; Figure 2 reverses the order to “Explainability & Interpretability”. This folder uses the table name.
