# O5 — Definition

[Table 6, p. 8, row O5 — Organizational goals](../../1-s2.0-S0164121224000980-main.pdf#page=8) introduces its definitions with “Addressing this concern involves specifying”:

> measurable benefits ML is expected to bring to the organization. E.g., increase the revenue in X%, increase the number of units sold in Y%, number of trees saved

## When to analyze it

Analyze Organizational goals when discussing [Set goals at different levels](../../perspectives/system-objectives/README.md#set-goals-at-different-levels) in the [System Objectives perspective](../../perspectives/system-objectives/). In the PerSpecML workflow, inspect the concern and its available relationships, determine whether it applies to the system, and then specify it if applicable. The task assignment identifies where to have this specification discussion; it does not require waiting until implementation.
