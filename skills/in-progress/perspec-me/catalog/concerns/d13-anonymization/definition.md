# D13 — Definition

[Table 10, p. 10, row D13 — Anonymization](../../1-s2.0-S0164121224000980-main.pdf#page=10) introduces its definitions with “Addressing this concern involves specifying”:

> the need to anonymize or pseudonymize to protect individual identities while still maintaining the utility of the data for ML purposes

## When to analyze it

Analyze Anonymization when discussing [Evaluate high-quality data](../../perspectives/data/README.md#evaluate-high-quality-data) in the [Data perspective](../../perspectives/data/). In the PerSpecML workflow, inspect the concern and its available relationships, determine whether it applies to the system, and then specify it if applicable. The task assignment identifies where to have this specification discussion; it does not require waiting until implementation.
