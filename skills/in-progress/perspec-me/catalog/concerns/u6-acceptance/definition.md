# U6 — Definition

[Table 7, p. 8, row U6 — Acceptance](../../1-s2.0-S0164121224000980-main.pdf#page=8) introduces its definitions with “Addressing this concern involves specifying”:

> how well and how the model arrives at its decisions

## When to analyze it

Analyze Acceptance when discussing [Ensure the credibility of predictions](../../perspectives/user-experience/README.md#ensure-the-credibility-of-predictions) in the [User Experience perspective](../../perspectives/user-experience/). In the PerSpecML workflow, inspect the concern and its available relationships, determine whether it applies to the system, and then specify it if applicable. The task assignment identifies where to have this specification discussion; it does not require waiting until implementation.
