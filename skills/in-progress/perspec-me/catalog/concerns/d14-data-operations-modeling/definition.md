# D14 — Definition

[Table 10, p. 10, row D14 — Data operations & Modeling](../../1-s2.0-S0164121224000980-main.pdf#page=10) introduces its definitions with “Addressing this concern involves specifying”:

> what operations must be applied on the data (e.g., data cleaning and labeling) and what is necessary to convert data in the representation of the model.

## When to analyze it

Analyze Data operations & Modeling when discussing [Convert data in the representation of the ML model](../../perspectives/data/README.md#convert-data-in-the-representation-of-the-ml-model) in the [Data perspective](../../perspectives/data/). In the PerSpecML workflow, inspect the concern and its available relationships, determine whether it applies to the system, and then specify it if applicable. The task assignment identifies where to have this specification discussion; it does not require waiting until implementation.

## Source differences

Table 10 uses “Data operations & Modeling”; Figure 2 uses “Data operations & modeling”. The table's capitalization is retained.
