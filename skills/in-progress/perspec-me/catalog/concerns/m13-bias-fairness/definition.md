# M13 — Definition

[Table 9, p. 9, row M13 — Bias & Fairness](../../1-s2.0-S0164121224000980-main.pdf#page=9) introduces its definitions with “Addressing this concern involves specifying”:

> the need for the model to treat different groups of people or entities

## When to analyze it

Analyze Bias & Fairness when discussing [Evaluate other quality characteristics](../../perspectives/model/README.md#evaluate-other-quality-characteristics) in the [Model perspective](../../perspectives/model/). In the PerSpecML workflow, inspect the concern and its available relationships, determine whether it applies to the system, and then specify it if applicable. The task assignment identifies where to have this specification discussion; it does not require waiting until implementation.

## Source differences

Table 9 ends the definition at “the need for the model to treat different groups of people or entities”. It does not specify how to treat those groups. The quotation is left intact; the introductory paraphrase also draws on section 3.4, p. 8, which discusses discrimination and negative consequences for groups.
