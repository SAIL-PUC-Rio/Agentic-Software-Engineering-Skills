# O10 — Definition

[Table 6, p. 8, row O10 — ML trade-off](../../1-s2.0-S0164121224000980-main.pdf#page=8) introduces its definitions with “Addressing this concern involves specifying”:

> the balance of customer expectations (e.g., inference time vs accuracy, false positive vs false negative)

## When to analyze it

Analyze ML trade-off when discussing [Manage expectations](../../perspectives/system-objectives/README.md#manage-expectations) in the [System Objectives perspective](../../perspectives/system-objectives/). In the PerSpecML workflow, inspect the concern and its available relationships, determine whether it applies to the system, and then specify it if applicable. The task assignment identifies where to have this specification discussion; it does not require waiting until implementation.
