# D11 — Definition

[Table 10, p. 10, row D11 — Consistency](../../1-s2.0-S0164121224000980-main.pdf#page=10) introduces its definitions with “Addressing this concern involves specifying”:

> the need to get consistent data in a specific context

## When to analyze it

Analyze Consistency when discussing [Evaluate high-quality data](../../perspectives/data/README.md#evaluate-high-quality-data) in the [Data perspective](../../perspectives/data/). In the PerSpecML workflow, inspect the concern and its available relationships, determine whether it applies to the system, and then specify it if applicable. The task assignment identifies where to have this specification discussion; it does not require waiting until implementation.

## Source differences

Table 10 assigns D11 to “Consistency”; Figure 2 assigns D11 to “Bias” and places “Consistency” at D9. This folder follows the table. Figure 2 references are preserved by their printed IDs; the difference is not silently repaired.
