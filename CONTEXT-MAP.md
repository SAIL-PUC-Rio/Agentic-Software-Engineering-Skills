# Context Map

## Contexts

- [Matt Pocock Skills](./CONTEXT.md) — the skills repo itself: buckets, issue trackers, evidence packs, triage roles
- [perspec-me](./skills/in-progress/perspec-me/CONTEXT.md) — PerSpecML-concern-guided wayfinding: Topics, Sessions, and a Map toward a Destination

## Relationships

- **perspec-me → Matt Pocock Skills**: a Topic held on the repo's **Issue tracker** *is* a **Decision ticket**; perspec-me reuses **Issue tracker**, **Issue**, and **Decision ticket** exactly as defined in the skills context rather than redefining them
- The two contexts share no types of their own: perspec-me's Concern, Topic, Session, and Map have no equivalent in the skills context, and the skills context's Evidence pack and Claim have no equivalent in perspec-me
